Privacy policy

Flow Breakdown for Mac · Last updated 18 September 2026

In short: Flow Breakdown doesn't collect anything. Your recordings, screens, notes and breakdowns stay on your Mac. There's no account, no analytics and no tracking.

What happens on your Mac

  • Finding screens in a recording runs on your Mac.
  • Voice notes are transcribed on your Mac with Apple's on-device speech recognition.
  • Key points and feeling suggestions use Apple Intelligence's on-device model.
  • Tap arrows (beta) are found on your Mac with Apple's Vision framework.

Flow Breakdown doesn't send any of this anywhere. macOS may download Apple's speech and Apple Intelligence models itself; that's between your Mac and Apple, under Apple's privacy policy.

What's stored, and where

Each breakdown is saved as a document in the folder you choose, with a copy of the recording you dropped, its screens, your notes and your mark-up. You can move, copy or delete these documents in Finder like any other file. The app also remembers its settings and the folder you chose, on your Mac.

What leaves your Mac

Only what you send yourself: when you copy to Figma or save an export, the content goes to your clipboard or to the file you pick. Flow Breakdown makes no network requests of its own.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with developers in macOS settings, Apple may pass anonymous crash reports to the developer. You can turn this off in System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Flow Breakdown collects no personal information from anyone, children included.
